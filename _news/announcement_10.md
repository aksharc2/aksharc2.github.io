**Paper accepted:** Our paper **“Per-object IoU Forecasting for Deadline-Aware Real-Time Embedded Detection Control”** (with Erfan Foorginejad and Marco Brocanelli) has been accepted at **ECCV 2026**. This work studies per-object detection accuracy degradation and deadline-aware runtime control for real-time embedded vision systems.
