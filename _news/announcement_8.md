**Paper update:** Our paper **“Fair Battery Degradation-Aware Edge Admission for Mobile Systems”** has been accepted as a **Regular Full Paper** at **IEEE EDGE 2026**. The work introduces a lightweight battery stress metric for mobile edge tasks, enabling admission decisions that account for latency, energy savings, and long-term battery degradation.
