**Paper accepted:** Our paper **“Speeding-up Graph Algorithms via Parallel Randomized Clique Partitioning”** has been accepted for publication in **IEEE Transactions on Parallel and Distributed Systems (TPDS)**. We introduce Par-RCP, a parallel randomized approach to lossless graph restructuring that reduces the number of edges while preserving path information and improving the efficiency of graph algorithms.
